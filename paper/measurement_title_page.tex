\section*{Prior presentation}
The study at a budget of 20 runs on four models was submitted as an
extended abstract to the IMEKO XXV World Congress (2027, TC21) [CONFIRM
status and reference at submission]; the present manuscript extends it to
twelve models, budgets of 10--80 runs, Bayesian and recalibrated GP
intervals, a cross-check against an independent implementation, a failure
mode, dependent inputs, vector outputs and a worked example with an
uncertainty budget.
